# Ashka Store Sales Dashboard (2018) — Power BI Project
This project analyzes sales performance for **Ashka Store**, an online retailer operating across India. 
The dashboard provides a clear view of revenue, profit, order trends, top-performing cities, product categories, and payment modes.

---

## Objectives
- Analyze sales performance across product categories and subcategories
- Identify top revenue-generating cities in India
- Understand monthly revenue and profit trends
- Compare revenue across payment modes
- Support the store owner with data-driven insights for better business decisions

---

## Key Insights
- **Total Revenue:** £437,771  
- **Total Profit:** £36,963  
- **Total Orders:** 500  
- **Top Category:** Electronics (37.98% of revenue)  
- **Top Subcategory:** Printers  
- **Top Cities by Revenue:** Indore, Mumbai, Pune  
- **Most Used Payment Mode:** Cash on Delivery (COD)  
- Revenue peaked in **January** and remained steady throughout the year
